# XEVORAS — Design Override: Avatar Studio & AI Try-On
**Page Routes:** `/avatar-studio` & `/virtual-try-on`
**Focus:** Immersive 3D Canvas · Responsive Controls · Privacy-Centric AI Vision

## Avatar Studio (`/avatar-studio`)
- Dark luxury studio setting (`#0d0d0d`) with three-point studio lighting.
- Left/Bottom control dock for body silhouette, skin tone, height, and garment categories.
- Real-time outfit rendering with 360-degree rotation and zoom orbit controls.
- Fast preset buttons: Front View, Side View, Back View, Detail Zoom.
- Commercial Actions: "Save Look", "Add Outfit to Bag".
- Clear fallback demo mode with high-fidelity visualizer when custom heavy 3D GLBs are loading.

## AI Virtual Try-On (`/virtual-try-on`)
- Privacy Shield banner: Explicit user consent required before image selection.
- Ephemeral photo processing disclaimer: No image training, automatic deletion.
- Clean 3-step workflow: 1. Upload Photo → 2. Select Garment → 3. Generate Look.
- Interactive Before & After comparison slider with high-performance touch dragging.
- Add-to-bag action integrated directly into result card.
